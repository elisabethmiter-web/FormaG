About the digital seal

Every signed PDF is digitally sealed. In Adobe Acrobat Reader, the Signatures panel shows whether the document was changed after it was signed.

Adobe may say the signer's identity is unknown or not verified. That only means the seal uses {{ platform_name }}'s own certificate rather than one bought from a certificate company; it doesn't affect the signatures. To make Adobe show the seal as trusted on your computer, download the certificate below, then in Adobe Reader open Preferences → Signatures → Identities & Trusted Certificates → Trusted Certificates → Import, choose the file and tick Use this certificate as a trusted root.
