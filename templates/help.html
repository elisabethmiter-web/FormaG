1. Send forms to a client

  1. Click New packet and type the client's name: clients you've sent to before are suggested, with their email.
  2. Tick the forms, or click a bundle to tick a set in one go. Add one-off files if needed.{% if has_approvals %} Add the SO# and sales rep for your records.{% endif %}
  3. Click ✉ Create and open in Email. A new email to your client opens by itself, with the subject, the message and the link to the forms already written in. Check it and press Send.

To send it again later, open the packet and click ✉ Open in Email. The ▾ next to it picks Outlook on the web, Gmail or your computer's email app. Want the link as a button named after your company and forms? Click Copy message and paste it into an empty email instead.

2. Text a client

  • On a phone: tap 💬 Text from my phone. Your Messages app opens with the text and link ready; pick the client and send.
  • On a computer: click 💬 Text from my phone and scan the code it shows with your phone's camera. Your phone opens a page with Open in Messages. The code works for 30 minutes. On a Mac you can also use Open Messages on this Mac.
  • Text messages show the full web address; that's normal for texts.

3. Client in front of you?

Click Sign now on this device (on New packet or any packet). The tablet or computer shows only the signing pages, so the client can't see anything else. When they're done, enter your password to get back to your screens. No access code or email is needed.

4. Two owners must sign

On New packet choose Two owners must both sign and add the second owner. Each owner gets their own link: send the second owner theirs from the Second owner section of the packet. The second signs after the first and sees what was filled in. The dashboard and Follow-up show "Waiting on …" so you know who to call. With Sign now on this device, both can sign one after the other.

5. Photos and files from the client

Tick Ask the client for photos or files and say what you need (for example "photos of your sink and faucet model numbers"). The client adds them from their phone or computer while signing. It's optional for them and never holds up the packet. Files appear on the packet page; you can add or change the request later from there.

6. Layouts and template drawings

  • Layout approval (Option A / B): add the stone and seam layout options; the client picks one and approves it.
  • Template drawing: send the drawing for approval. Each updated drawing gets the next revision (Rev 1, Rev 2…) with a note on what changed. The client approves it or asks for changes.

{{ 7 if has_approvals else 6 }}. Keep track

  • The Packets page shows Sent → Opened → In progress → Signed. New since your last visit lists what changed, and the menu shows a count.
  • Click Turn on alerts on the Packets page to get a pop-up when a client opens or signs, while the app is open in a tab.
  • Follow-up lists who needs a nudge today: overdue, not opened in 2 days, opened but not finished, or waiting on the second owner. Each has the reminder ready in one click; once you've reminded someone, they move to Already reminded today.
  • Clients have 48 hours to sign; late packets turn orange. Record calls and notes in Follow-ups & notes on each packet.
  • Times show in your company's time zone.

{{ 8 if has_approvals else 7 }}. Signed copies

  • When everyone has signed, the packet shows Signed. Download all gives one ZIP with the signed PDFs, the audit trail and the client's files.
  • Clients download theirs from their link at any time; ask them to keep it.
  • Every signed PDF is digitally sealed. Anyone can check one is genuine and unchanged at {{ url_for('verify', _external=True) }}. Adobe may say the signer's identity is "unknown": that's expected, and the signing record page explains it.

{{ 9 if has_approvals else 8 }}. Keeping documents private

Tick Require an access code on New packet (or turn it on from the packet). The client must enter a 4-digit code to open their link. Give it by phone or in person, never in the same message as the link. After 5 wrong tries the link waits 15 minutes. You can make a new code or turn it off on the packet page.

{{ 10 if has_approvals else 9 }}. For managers

  • Form library: upload PDFs or Word/Excel files, or build fillable forms. Place signature puts Signature, Name and Date boxes exactly where they belong on a PDF.
  • Bundles: save sets of forms you send together (for example "Kitchen job").
  • Team: add staff and managers, reset a password, or remove someone.
  • Company: your company name, logo and colour (shown to clients), time zone, and storage used.
  • Export to Excel on the Packets page: every packet with its status and dates, for reports.

{{ 11 if has_approvals else 10 }}. Your account

Click your name at the top to change your password and choose where Open in Email opens: the email app on your computer (Outlook, Apple Mail), Outlook on the web (Microsoft 365), Gmail, or Outlook.com. Open your account.

{{ 13 if has_approvals else 12 }}. Common problems

  • The email didn't open? Click ✉ Open in Email on the packet. If you use Outlook on the web or Gmail, allow pop-ups for this site when your browser asks.
  • Text from my phone does nothing on a computer? Use the code it shows and scan it with your phone.
  • Forgot your password? Ask a manager to reset it from the Team page.
  • Client lost the link or it expired? Open the packet, click Extend link if needed, and send it again.
  • Client can't open the link? If it asks for a code, give them the access code from the packet page.
  • Page says the service is restarting? Wait a minute: the client's signature stays on the page and is sent automatically.
