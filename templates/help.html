1. Send forms to a client

  1. Click New packet and type the client's name: clients you've sent to before are suggested, with their email.
  2. Tick the forms, or click a bundle to tick a set in one go. Add one-off files if needed.{% if has_approvals %} Add the SO# and sales rep for your records.{% endif %}
  3. Click ✉ Create and open in Email. A new email to your client opens with the subject and message written in.
  4. Make the link clickable: click in the message, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+V (⌘A, ⌘V on a Mac). The text is replaced by the same message with a clickable button named after your company and the forms (for example "{{ g.company.name if g.company else 'Your company' }} – Deposit Terms"). Press Send. This works in Outlook, Gmail, Yahoo and Apple Mail.

To send it again later, open the packet and click ✉ Open in Email. If you use Outlook on the web or Gmail in your browser, pick it from the ▾ next to the button. Copy message copies the clickable version again if you need it.

Job details: if a form has job-detail boxes (for example the site address or colour), New packet asks you to fill them in, and they're printed on the form before your client sees it. Check them on the confirmation screen: they can't be changed after the packet is created (make a new packet instead).

2. Text a client

  • On a phone: tap 💬 Text from my phone. Your Messages app opens with the text and link ready; pick the client and send.
  • On a computer: click 💬 Text from my phone and scan the code it shows with your phone's camera. Your phone opens a page with Open in Messages. The code works for 30 minutes. On a Mac you can also use Open Messages on this Mac.
  • Text messages show the full web address; that's normal for texts.

3. Client in front of you?

Click Sign now on this device (on New packet or any packet). The tablet or computer shows only the signing pages, so the client can't see anything else. When they're done, enter your password to get back to your screens. No access code or email is needed.

4. Two owners must sign

On New packet choose Two owners must both sign and add the second owner. Each owner gets their own link: send the second owner theirs from the Second owner section of the packet. The second signs after the first and sees what was filled in. The dashboard and Follow-up show "Waiting on …" so you know who to call. With Sign now on this device, both can sign one after the other.

5. Photos and files from the client

Tick Ask the client for photos or files and say what you need (for example "photos of your sink and faucet model numbers"). The client adds them from their phone or computer while signing. It's optional for them and never holds up the packet. Files appear on the packet page; you can add or change the request later from there.

{{ 7 if has_approvals else 6 }}. Keep track

  • The Packets page is live: it updates by itself when a client opens or signs (a pop-up at the bottom says who), results appear as you type in the search box, and clicking a column heading sorts by it.
  • Charts: click a row in Where packets are, or one of the number cards to show just those packets; click again (or the × on the filter) to show all. Typical time to sign shows how fast your clients sign.
  • On each row: Email opens the email (or a reminder) for that client, Copy link copies their signing link, Archive hides a finished packet. Click anywhere else on a row for a quick preview: forms, who signed, recent activity.
  • Several at once: tick the boxes to Remind… them one after another, Export to Excel, or Archive them (with Undo).
  • The Packets page shows Sent → Opened → In progress → Signed. New since your last visit lists what changed, and the menu shows a count.
  • Click Turn on alerts on the Packets page to get a pop-up when a client opens or signs, while the app is open in a tab.
  • Follow-up lists who needs a nudge today: overdue, not opened in 2 days, opened but not finished, or waiting on the second owner. Each has the reminder ready in one click; once you've reminded someone, they move to Already reminded today.
  • Clients have 48 hours to sign; late packets turn orange. Record calls and notes in Follow-ups & notes on each packet: type a note (its box ticks itself) and click Save follow-ups. Sending a reminder from the app (Send a reminder, Open in Email or Text from my phone) fills in the next follow-up for you, for example "Reminder sent to Sam Lee by email". The latest follow-up and your notes show in the Follow-up column on the Packets page and in the quick preview.
  • Times show in your company's time zone.
  • More ▾ on a packet: Same forms, another client (or Send again with changes for the same client) fills in New packet for you; Edit client's name or email fixes a typo without making a new packet; Archive hides a finished packet from the dashboard (nothing is deleted; see the Archived tab, or search).

{{ 8 if has_approvals else 7 }}. Signed copies

Clients can stop and come back: their answers are saved as they type, and Save and finish later lets them close the page and finish from the same link, even on another device. At the bottom of their pages they see who to contact with questions.

  • When everyone has signed, the packet shows Signed. Download all gives one ZIP with the signed PDFs, the audit trail and the client's files.
  • Clients download theirs from their link at any time; ask them to keep it.
  • Every signed PDF is digitally sealed. Anyone can check one is genuine and unchanged at {{ url_for('verify', _external=True) }}. Adobe may say the signer's identity is "unknown": that's expected, and the signing record page explains it.

{{ 9 if has_approvals else 8 }}. Keeping documents private

Tick Require an access code on New packet (or turn it on from the packet). The client must enter a 4-digit code to open their link. Give it by phone or in person, never in the same message as the link. After 5 wrong tries the link waits 15 minutes. You can make a new code or turn it off on the packet page.

{{ 10 if has_approvals else 9 }}. For managers

  • Form library: upload PDFs or Word/Excel files, or build fillable forms. Place signature puts Signature, Name and Date boxes exactly where they belong on a PDF.
  • Initials, job details and "I've read this page": in Place signature, add Initials boxes (the client initials each one), Job details boxes that your team fills in on New packet, and an I've read this page tick on any page. You can also type {Placeholder} words in a built form's text (for example {Site address}) to ask for a job detail. The signing record lists every initial and tick with its time.
  • {% if is_manager %} {% endif %}
  • Bundles: save sets of forms you send together (for example "Kitchen job").
  • Team: each person's card shows packets sent, signed and overdue this month (click it to see their packets). Add someone with an invite link: they choose their own password. Click a name, email or access level to change it; the switch turns sign-in off or on (with Undo). More ▾ moves someone's open packets to another person.
  • Company: your company name, logo and colour (shown to clients), time zone, storage used, the "Need help?" line your clients see (phone and email), and the message wording your team's emails and texts use. A live preview shows what clients see as you type, and Send a test to myself lets you go through a packet exactly like a client.
  • Keeping and clearing old packets (Company page): choose to move finished packets to Archived after some months, and to delete them for good after some years. Deleting is off unless you turn it on; the page lists what's due to be deleted in the next 30 days, and keeps a record (reference and dates only) of what was deleted. Deleted packets can't be recovered.
  • Delete a packet permanently: on the packet, More ▾ → Delete packet permanently…, tick the box and confirm. The packet, its signed PDFs, signatures and files from the client are removed for good and can't be recovered, so click Download all first if you need a copy. Only management can do this; to just hide a packet, use Archive.
  • Export to Excel on the Packets page: every packet with its status and dates, for reports.

{{ 11 if has_approvals else 10 }}. Your account

Click your name at the top to change your password (use Show to check what you typed; a meter shows how strong it is). On a shared computer, leave Stay signed in unticked at sign-in: you're signed out when the browser closes or after {{ (idle_minutes // 60) if idle_minutes >= 60 else idle_minutes }} {{ 'hours' if idle_minutes >= 120 else ('hour' if idle_minutes >= 60 else 'minutes') }} without activity, with a warning a minute before. Open in Email uses the email app set as the default on your computer (for example Outlook). If nothing opens, set your default email app in your computer's settings, or use the ▾ next to the button to open Outlook on the web or Gmail instead.

  • Quick search: press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac), or /, or click Search at the top, and type a client's name{% if has_approvals %}, an SO#{% endif %}, a form or a page. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open one; New packet for… starts a packet with that client filled in.
  • Install the app: on a computer (Chrome or Edge) or an Android phone, click Install app at the top (or search for it with Ctrl+K), or use the browser menu's Install option. On an iPhone or iPad, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens in its own window with its own icon, and shows a "You're offline" page if the connection drops.
  • ✨ What's new at the top shows recent improvements to the app; a number shows how many you haven't read.

{{ 13 if has_approvals else 12 }}. Common problems

  • The email didn't open? Your computer may not have a default email app. Open the packet and use the ▾ next to ✉ Open in Email to choose Outlook on the web or Gmail, or set Outlook as the default email app in your computer's settings.
  • Text from my phone does nothing on a computer? Use the code it shows and scan it with your phone.
  • Forgot your password? Ask a manager to reset it from the Team page.
  • Client lost the link or it expired? Open the packet, click Extend link if needed, and send it again.
  • Client can't open the link? If it asks for a code, give them the access code from the packet page.
  • Page says the service is restarting? Wait a minute: the client's signature stays on the page and is sent automatically.
